# Appendix — index of figures
> every figure · the sheet it lives in · what it shows

The figures are numbered across the whole document rather than per sheet ([A3.8](sc_a3#a38)), and each one lives in the sheet it illustrates — so this is the only place they can be seen as a set. The three build phases on [P1](sec_ai_p1), [P2](sec_ai_p2) and [P3](sec_ai_p3) are one drawing in three states, each with that sheet's own phase picked out and the other two held back; they carry three numbers of their own rather than one number with a letter hung off it. The glossary is [Z3](sec_ai_z3).

The list below is not written by hand. A caption declares an identifier — `![F.memmap.Memory structure…]` — and the prose points at that identifier rather than at a number, so `tools/numbers.js` can assign the numbering across the reading order and inserting a figure renumbers the set without a single cross-reference being touched. In the printed edition every row also carries the page its figure falls on.
