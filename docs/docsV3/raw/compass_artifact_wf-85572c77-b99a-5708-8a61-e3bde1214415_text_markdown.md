# noVa64 (DANI-65816) — Prototype Phase Execution Plan to the "Apple II Milestone"

## TL;DR
- The plan is executable as written: go straight to the Stage B four-layer carrier PCB, reach an autonomous boot-to-prompt in 12 gates (E0–E5, K0–K5), and the single hardest schedule risk is the first-spin carrier board debugged without any wire-wrap validation — mitigated by heavy test-point/socket/strap provisioning and a staged power-on procedure.
- Three of your open items are resolved by research: (5) the PCM5102A **32fs BCK/LRCK ratio IS valid in PLL mode** for sample rates ≥32 kHz with 16-bit data (32fs is the minimum ratio; 48 kHz→1.536 MHz BCK), so your clock architecture stands; (6) MBR type bytes **0x7A/0x7B are NOT safe** — use **0x7F** (reserved for the Alternative OS Development Partition Standard) instead; and a newly discovered blocker — (new) the Colorlight i9's onboard SDRAM has **CKE/CS#/DQM tied to VCC/GND and not driven by the FPGA**, so it cannot serve as your byte-maskable per-FPGA SDRAM and you must place your own SDRAM on the carrier.
- Two internal inconsistencies to fix before schematic capture: the Calypsi toolchain uses **32-bit far pointers by default** (24-bit is the `__far24` qualifier, not the default), and the SD block register map plus the Debug-Agent BE/DBG_CSN/CDC-crossing items are the true critical path — sequence them first (tasks T10–T12) because they gate all NVFS bring-up and therefore autonomous boot.

## Key Findings

### Resolved open items
1. **PCM5102A 32fs question (open item #5) — RESOLVED, your assumption holds.** The current TI datasheet is revision **SLAS859C (May 2012, revised May 2015)**, "PCM510xA 2.1 VRMS, 112/106/100 dB Audio Stereo DAC with PLL and 32-bit, 384 kHz PCM Interface." Its BCK-rate table lists only two BCK columns, **32fs and 64fs**. 32fs is valid for all sample rates from 32 kHz upward (44.1 kHz→1.4112 MHz; 48 kHz→1.536 MHz; 96 kHz→3.072 MHz). The datasheet constraint is that **32fs BCK is legal only with 16-bit audio data**; verbatim: *"Audio data word = 16-bit, BCK = 32, 48, 64fS; Audio data word = 24-bit, BCK = 48, 64fS; Audio data word = 32-bit, BCK = 64fS."* Since NEON's APU is specified as 4-channel 16-bit PCM, 32fs is compatible. **Recommendation:** lock the format as I²S, 16-bit, 48 kHz, 32fs (BCK=1.536 MHz, LRCK=48 kHz), SCK grounded, PLL mode. Critical caution, verbatim from SLAS859C: *"If the relationship between LRCK and BCK are invalid more than 4 LRCK periods, internal operation is initialized within one sample period and analog outputs are forced to the bipolar zero level until resynchronization between LRCK and BCK is completed."* Therefore the APU must emit continuous, glitch-free BCK/LRCK even when idle (send zero-PCM; do not gate the clocks). Also note 8 kHz supports neither 32fs nor 64fs, and 16 kHz supports only 64fs — avoid those two rates.
2. **NVFS partition type 0x7A/0x7B (open item #6) — NOT SAFE; change them.** On Wikipedia's canonical "Partition type" table, 0x7A is the hidden-variant alias of 0x0E (hidden FAT16 LBA) and 0x7B is the hidden-variant alias of 0x06 (hidden FAT16B); in Andries Brouwer's canonical list both are simply absent (the list jumps 0x78→0x7E). So they are "unclaimed" but semantically collide with the "hidden-FAT" (+0x10) convention, meaning some tools will silently treat an NVFS partition as a hidden FAT. **Recommendation: use 0x7F.** Per Wikipedia's "Partition type" article: *"any new temporary partition type assignments for local or experimental projects can utilize type 7Fh in order to avoid conflicts with already assigned types. This type was specially reserved for individual use as part of the Alternative OS Development Partition Standard (AODPS) initiative since 2002."* Avoid 0x8x (Linux/Minix ranges) and 0xAx (Apple/FreeBSD). Register your choice in the NVFS spec and in mkfs.nvfs so nvfsck warns on a mismatch.

### Newly discovered blocker (flag)
3. **The Colorlight i9's onboard M12L64322A SDRAM is not usable as your design's byte-maskable per-FPGA SDRAM.** DatanoiseTV's colorlight-i9-abletonlink project states verbatim that the SDRAM (chip "M12L64322A 8 MB / 32-bit / 4-bank") has *"CKE/CS#/DQM[0..3] tied to VCC/GND/GND — not driven."* This is corroborated by the chubby75/q3k Colorlight reverse-engineering notes: *"the SDRAM ~CS/~LDQM/~UDQM are connected to GND and CKE connected to 3v3."* Consequences: (a) no per-byte write masking is possible on the onboard SDRAM (every write is a full 32-bit word), and (b) CS#/CKE cannot be de-asserted. Your memory model requires byte-addressable, DQM-controlled SDRAM (SRAM is a byte-granular cache over SDRAM frames). Therefore the onboard i9 SDRAM can at best serve as coarse scratch/framebuffer during early bring-up; the real 64 MB AS4C32M16SB / IS42S16320F SDRAM must be placed on the carrier PCB and wired to spare FPGA I/O, exactly as your production design intends. This actually *reduces* the risk of the Stage-A cancellation because it forces the SDRAM interface onto the carrier where you control DQM/CS#. Confirm against the i9 v7.2 pin map (and a continuity check) before committing.

### Internal inconsistencies to fix
4. **Calypsi pointer model.** Your context says "24-bit pointers." Calypsi's default far data pointer is **32 bits** (4 bytes). Confirmed by the Area 73 developer writeup on Calypsi C for the 65816: *"The __near qualifier tells the Calypsi 65816 compiler that this pointer is within the current data bank… implemented in just 16 bits. There are also __tiny, __far24, and __far, which mark pointers as 8-bit (direct page), 24-bit, or 32-bit."* (Calypsi is authored by Håkan Thörngren / hth313.) This matters for your ABI, struct layout and kernel/driver bank-crossing conventions. Decide per-object which qualifier to use and document it; don't assume "24-bit by default."
5. **i9 FPGA package is caBGA-381 (LFE5U-45F-6BG381C), speed grade −6.** Your "BGA is the only prohibited package" rule applies to parts *you* solder; the i9 arrives pre-assembled so this is fine, but note (a) you never get to probe FPGA balls directly — all debug must go through module SODIMM pins, reinforcing the test-point requirement, and (b) speed grade −6 is the slowest ECP5 bin; budget static-timing margin accordingly (your 102 MHz NEON pixel-clock domain is achievable on −6 but verify in nextpnr with real constraints early).

### Part errata / gotchas (condensed; full list in Details)
- **W65C816S:** the multiplexed bank address on D0–D7 during PHI2-low needs an external transparent latch (74AC/74LVC '573 class, ≤10 ns), latched on PHI2/RDY low; at 14 MHz the valid window is ~12 ns. Qualify all chip-selects with VDA∨VPA. RDY is bidirectional and is pulled low by WAI — your Helium arbiter/debug-halt logic must treat RDY as I/O, not input. ABORT cannot prevent a user WAI/STP hang (privilege gap) — plan a watchdog. Bank bits are "don't care" when VDA∨VPA=0.
- **ECP5/nextpnr:** bidirectional/tristate signals must be tri-stated only at the top level (Trellis limitation); PLL outputs are not always auto-promoted to global clock nets — check nextpnr logs and force via DCCA if needed; consult Lattice TN-02200 (sysCLOCK PLL) and the ECP5 Hardware Checklist (FPGA-TN-02038).
- **RP2040:** errata **RP2040-E5** (USB enumeration can fail when two CDC devices are present / on cold boot) — use the pico-sdk `rp2040_usb_device_enumeration` fix; slow-rising supplies can wedge boot (not an erratum, a spec fact) — ensure a clean RUN reset and monotonic 3V3_AON ramp; keep the boot2 flash clock ≤50 MHz for the W25Q-class flash.
- **PCM5102A:** XSMT must be a driven GPIO (open item #4 is correct — do not leave on an RC strap); clocks must be continuous or the DAC mutes/re-inits.

### C. Recommended first-spin bring-up procedure (no prior wire-wrap validation)
1. **Bare board:** visual + continuity; check every rail net for shorts to GND before any power.
2. **Rails only, current-limited:** bench PSU at each rail's voltage with a low current limit, FPGA module and CPU absent. Bring up 3V3_AON first (feeds RP2040/flash/CH224K/charger I²C only), verify, then each EN-gated rail one at a time by manually asserting its EN, checking voltage and inrush on the shunt/0Ω. Confirm VPP_2V5 via BAT54 and CH224K 9V negotiation.
3. **RP2040 alone:** populate RP2040 + flash; confirm boot (BOOTSEL/UF2), USB-CDC enumeration (apply E5 fix), and that firmware can toggle each EN and read the charger/fuel-gauge I²C and GT911 on its dedicated bus.
4. **FPGA configuration:** insert i9 module; RP2040/JTAG configures a blinky; verify with openFPGALoader `--detect` (expect ECP5 LFE5U-45 idcode ≈0x41111043).
5. **Clocks:** load a PLL test bitstream; scope every derived clock; confirm nextpnr promoted clocks to globals; verify the 102 MHz NEON domain and I²S BCK/LRCK continuity.
6. **SRAM** then **carrier SDRAM:** memory tests (walking 1s, address-in-address, marching-C). Verify DQM/CS#/CKE actually toggle on the carrier SDRAM (the whole reason it isn't the onboard part).
7. **CPU:** socket the W65C816S last; generate PHI2 (start at a jumper-selectable low clock, 2–4 MHz); scope the bank-address latch window; run a ca65 loop from SRAM; hand control between CPU and Debug Agent via BE.
8. **Video:** verify VGA RGB666 R-2R output; text Mode 0.

### F. Detailed part errata / gotchas
- **W65C816S:** Bank address is multiplexed onto D0–D7 during PHI2-low and must be captured by an external transparent latch (74AC/74LVC '573 class), latched on PHI2/RDY low; per community analysis of the WDC datasheet, at 14 MHz the valid latch window is ~12 ns, so use a ≤10 ns part. Qualify every chip-select with VDA∨VPA (both low = "dead" bus cycle, bank bits are don't-care). RDY is bidirectional and pulled low internally by WAI (also on RESB/IRQB/NMIB/ABORTB) — treat it as I/O in Helium. ABORT cannot stop a user WAI/STP hang (a known privilege gap discussed on 6502.org) — add a watchdog. M/X output is invalid during the cycle after REP/SEP/PLP.
- **Colorlight i9 v7.2:** FPGA is LFE5U-45F-6BG381C (caBGA-381, −6 speed). Community pinout maps exist (wuxx/Colorlight-FPGA-Projects; DatanoiseTV; chubby75). Onboard SDRAM is ESMT **M12L64322A** (8 MB, 512K×32×4-bank) with **CKE/CS#/DQM tied off, not FPGA-driven** — unusable for byte-masked access (finding #3). JTAG pins J27/J31/J32/J30 (TCK/TMS/TDI/TDO); 25 MHz clock on P3; LED on L2.
- **ECP5 / nextpnr / Trellis:** tristate/bidirectional nets must be resolved only at top level (Trellis limitation); PLL outputs are not always promoted to globals — check logs, force via DCCA; use TN-02200 for PLL, FPGA-TN-02038 hardware checklist. openFPGALoader has occasional bitstream/detach/JTAG-chain-ordering quirks — keep ecpprog + DAPLink as fallbacks.
- **RP2040:** RP2040-E5 USB enumeration erratum — use pico-sdk `rp2040_usb_device_enumeration` fix; slow-rising supplies can wedge boot; keep boot2 flash clock ≤50 MHz.
- **PCM5102A:** slave-only (LRCK/BCK are inputs); XSMT must be a driven GPIO; clocks must be continuous (mutes after 4 invalid LRCK periods).
- **IS61WV102416 / AS4C32M16SB:** standard async SRAM / SDR SDRAM; verify OE/WE only asserted during PHI2-high for the CPU-side path; length-match SDRAM byte lanes.
- **BQ25896 / MAX17048 / CH224K / GT911:** BQ25896 needs correct SYS behaviour with no battery present (see TI E2E FAQ); MAX17048 (1S) senses at CELL, I²C ≤400 kHz; GT911 INT must be floating when in input state and RESET held low ≥ spec during power-up (the INT/RESET timing sets the I²C address) — its dedicated RP2040 I²C bus is the right call to avoid AON backfeed.

## Recommendations
1. **Do the four design-freeze corrections first (T04/T05):** partition byte → 0x7F; Calypsi pointer model documented as 32-bit default (`__far24` for 24-bit); carrier SDRAM mandatory; audio locked to 16-bit/48 kHz/32fs PLL mode. Cheap, unblock everything, remove three latent inconsistencies.
2. **Sequence the SD block map (T11) and Debug-Agent items (T10/T12) as the true critical path** — they gate NVFS-on-hardware and therefore the unattended-boot milestone, and they block schematic capture. Do them before board layout.
3. **Spend the Stage-A savings on DFM-for-debug (T18) and good instruments (DSLogic U3Pro16 + Rigol DHO-series 12-bit scope).** This is the direct trade that makes a first-spin-only strategy survivable.
4. **Lay GUI groundwork only where cheap (T27):** keep cell-buffer scanout, damage rectangles, and compositor plumbing present but dormant; do not build the Amiga milestone now.
5. **Benchmarks that change the plan:** if the first spin fails power or CPU bring-up in a way not fixable with jumpers, commit to a rev-B rather than bodging (threshold: >2 cut-and-jump fixes on power, or any bank-latch fix requiring a new net). If nextpnr cannot close 102 MHz on the −6 part after pipelining, drop the NEON pixel clock or use Diamond for that module. If EBR for any feature would push the *portable* iCE40 budget over 32 blocks, cut the feature now, not later.
