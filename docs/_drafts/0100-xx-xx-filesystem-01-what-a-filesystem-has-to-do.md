## What a filesystem has to do

Notes to self on the shape of a post in a series:

- `categories:` is the track — one, always. Here, storage.
- `series:` and `part:` are what put it in reading order. Parts are ordered by
  `part`, not by date, so a part can be slotted in between two published ones
  later. Gaps are fine, duplicates are not.
- `tags:` is the fine-grained axis — fat32, allocator, sd — and is free-form.

Delete all of the above before publishing.
